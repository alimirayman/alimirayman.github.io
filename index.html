Shastho Limited

Bangladesh's healthcare system faces deep accessibility and infrastructure challenges – nearly 65% of people with medical needs never reach formal healthcare facilities, let alone digital platforms. That reality led me to found Shastho Limited to digitize and connect healthcare through technology.

Building Bangladesh's first holistic digital health ecosystem integrating AI-driven automation, lab networks, doctor coordination, and corporate health programs.

  • Strategic Partnerships: Collaborating with leading healthcare providers including Samorita Hospital, BRAC Healthcare, and Aichi Healthcare Group.
  • Product Suite: Shastho AI (automated health intelligence), Shastho Card (affordable membership access), Shastho Elite (concierge-style care), Shastho Corporate (employee wellness programs).
  • Architecture: Built on AWS and Node.js with event-sourced local-first architecture for data integrity and real-time synchronization.
  • Mission: Making healthcare accessible, intelligent, and convenient for every household in Bangladesh.

BusinessOS

Most small businesses in Bangladesh track finances on paper or WhatsApp. That friction is expensive – in errors, wasted time, and decisions made without clear data.

As a side project, I'm building BusinessOS – a local-first ERP and desktop SaaS that makes business finance simple and affordable for entrepreneurs and SMEs.

  • Technology Stack: Built on an offline-first architecture using Electron, Nuxt, and SQLite with event-sourced data models.
  • Key Advantage: Small businesses can operate reliably without needing constant internet connectivity – critical in markets with unreliable infrastructure.
  • Vision: Democratizing business finance tools that were previously only accessible to large enterprises.
